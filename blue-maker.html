Project · Blue Mind Life Style

The Blue Maker

Blue Mind Life Style is Florencia’s ongoing body of work — painting, textile and interior objects built around a single conviction: that proximity to blue and to water measurably restores calm, focus and wellbeing. Each collection below approaches that idea from a different sense.

mind

Relax · Peace · Creativity · Cheerful · Freedom — the emotional register of the work, in acrylic and watercolour.

body

Wool and woven pieces — Okinawa, Barbagia, Loma Linda, Icaria, Nicoya — named for the world’s Blue Zones of longevity.

spirit

Pacífico, Ártico, Índico, Atlántico, Antártico — large-scale textured canvases named for the oceans that inspire them.

soul

Sunlight, Twilight, Midlight, Abyss, Freedom — the deepest, most introspective works in the collection.

“In Florencia Zampieri’s work there is an infinite search between the intense and the subtle. Reinterpreting everything she creates is entering a universe full of questions and challenges.”
Vivian Guggenheim — Visual Artist, Madrid
