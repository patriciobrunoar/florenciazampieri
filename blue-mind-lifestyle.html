Project · Blue Mind Life Style

Blue Mind Life Style

Blue Mind Life Style is Florencia’s ongoing body of work — painting, textile and interior objects built around a single conviction: that proximity to blue and to water measurably restores calm, focus and wellbeing. Each of the five collections below approaches that idea from a different sense.

mind

Relax · Peace · Creativity · Cheerful · Freedom — the emotional register of the work, in acrylic and watercolour.

body

Wool and woven pieces — Okinawa, Barbagia, Loma Linda, Icaria, Nicoya — named for the world’s Blue Zones of longevity.

spirit

Pacífico, Ártico, Índico, Atlántico, Antártico — large-scale textured canvases named for the oceans that inspire them.

soul

Sunlight, Twilight, Midlight, Abyss, Freedom — the deepest, most introspective works in the collection.

sense

Twelve senses in three registers — bodily, emotional and cognitive. Inhabiting the Sense, making visible what seems invisible.

“In Florencia Zampieri’s work there is an infinite search between the intense and the subtle. Reinterpreting everything she creates is entering a universe full of questions and challenges.”
Vivian Guggenheim — Visual Artist, Madrid

Collection · Blue Senses

Blue Senses

Blue Senses is inhabiting the Sense — making visible what appears to be invisible; understanding that sensory attention is an experience that forms part of our inner life. Entering the weave of the Twelve Senses was a way of meeting my own sense of perception again.

The series follows Rudolf Steiner’s study of the relationship between thinking and the perception of the world, and the anthroposophical reading of the senses as the functions through which the perceiving self reaches the world outside it. Twelve works, one for each sense, gathered into three registers.

Inspired by that philosophy and in tune with it, I find calm in the palette of blue tones. My mind expands, the creative process opens, and through that serenity I give form to my work.

bodily

Sentidos Corporales — perception of one’s own interiority.

Tacto · Vida · Movimiento · Equilibrio

emotional

Sentidos Emocionales — perception of the world.

Olfato · Gusto · Vista · Térmico

cognitive

Sentidos Cognitivos — perception of the interiority of the other.

Auditivo · Palabra Ajena · Pensamiento Ajeno · Yo Ajeno

Finding beauty is being attentive to what life shows us, being able to listen to silence in order to connect with each of our senses — and, more importantly, with the sense of the other, the one who returns our gaze, who perceives my work, who opens their soul, frees their thinking, feels and empathises with the depths of my blue tones. I invite contemplation, the transfer of feeling, experimentation; to become more human, to quieten ourselves and listen to ourselves, to free each of our senses in an aesthetic, watercolour-like and continuous flow, within the blue spectrum of Blue Mind Life Style.
