The Artist

Statement

I am an artist who transforms spaces, an architect who is inspired through movement, a dancer who draws, light, in the air, when she rises. The canvas is a pentagram, which allows me to listen, perceive and connect with other canvases, other emotions. Art in me is inevitable, it is magic that materializes the human essence, the transforming alchemy of the body, spirit and soul.

The structure was my starting point, my axis of balance for dancing and composing. I learned to dematerialize without losing harmony, always attracted by the creative discipline and experimentation, the plastic, abstract and geometric language of the Bauhaus School but at the same time, poetic, appealing to metaphor and the re-signification of the element and form. I am inspired by places I visit, cities near water, I am inspired by the beauty of calm, the nature of simplicity, textures and their depths, subjective aesthetics, the magic of the unexpected, the books I read. I don’t find limits between my perceptions, I think they intertwine with each other and allow the fluidity I need to create.

I seek to inhabit beauty, let myself be carried away by the unexpected, materialize sound and emotions, create affinity with the other, generate movement to whom my art reaches in the depths of its essence.

Biography

Florencia Zampieri

Architect · Neuroarchitect · Visual Artist · University Professor · Wellbeing Researcher · Method Creator

Florencia Zampieri is an Architect and Urban Planner, Neuroarchitect, Visual Artist, University Professor, Wellbeing Researcher, and creator of innovative methodologies integrating architecture, neuroscience, perception, color, art, learning, wellbeing, and longevity.

With more than 30 years of professional and academic experience, her work explores the relationship between people and the environments they inhabit, investigating how the built environment can influence perception, emotions, behavior, learning, and wellbeing.

She holds a degree in Architecture and Urban Planning from the Faculty of Architecture, Design and Urbanism of the University of Buenos Aires and has specialized in Neuroarchitecture, Cognitive Neuroscience, and Environmental Psychology. She has been involved in university teaching since 1996.

Her artistic journey began at an early age and includes formal training as a Classical Dance Teacher. This background in movement, body awareness, spatial perception, and artistic expression has profoundly influenced her approach to architecture and research, particularly in exploring the relationship between body, space, movement, emotion, and perception.

She is the founder and creator of The Blue Maker, a platform dedicated to education, research, consulting, and professional development in architecture, design, color, neuroarchitecture, and wellbeing. She is also the creator of the MINC Method and develops research and tools focused on Neuro-Compatible Spaces — Blue Nodes — environments intentionally designed to respond to people’s cognitive, emotional, sensory, and social needs.

Her research into the color blue led to the development of Blue Move® & Blue Mind Life Style, a line of work exploring the relationship between the sea, color, perception, the brain, and human wellbeing. Her work in this field has been presented through artistic, academic, and research projects, based on the Blue Mind theory of Dr. Wallace J. Nichols.

Florencia is an active advocate and communicator of the Conscious Longevity Movement, promoting programs inspired by quality of life, active aging, and international research developed together with her global partners. Her work connects urban planning and future-oriented design with the needs of longevity-oriented populations and the Silver Economy, proposing new ways of understanding cities, communities, workplaces, and the built environment throughout the life course.

Her strategic vision of human development, talent management, and the future of work also led her to become Co-Founder of Talent Ally Group, an international initiative focused on recognizing and developing innate talent and creating more human-centered organizational cultures.

In recent years, her work has evolved toward an integrated approach to longevity and the built environment, developing concepts such as Blue Ageless, Learning Architecture, Neuroarts, and the Longevity Economy. Her approach moves beyond conventional notions of aging and proposes new ways of designing spaces, cities, and systems that support people throughout the entire life course.

Florencia Zampieri is currently LATAM Ambassador for Art and Culture at Living Blue Zone International, promoting the integration of art, culture, design, and knowledge as tools for creating more active, connected, meaningful, and longevity-conscious communities.

As a visual artist, she has participated in exhibitions in Asia, Europe, Latin America, USA and the United Arab Emirates, developing an artistic practice centered on color, perception, nature, and sensory experience. Her Blue Immersion project further explores these themes by transforming blue into an immersive spatial and perceptual experience.

She is a speaker, researcher, educator, and author whose work focuses on the intersection of architecture, neuroscience, art, color, wellbeing, learning, and longevity. She also contributes to public dissemination and media through articles and columns addressing contemporary trends in wellbeing and the built environment.

Florencia currently works as a consultant, researcher, educator, speaker, and scientific and cultural communicator, presenting publications, academic papers, conferences, and articles through national and international platforms. She is a member of the European Society of Medicine (ESMED) and the Academy of Neuroscience for Architecture (ANFA).

Her purpose is to transform the way we think about, design, and inhabit our environments, understanding architecture not simply as physical infrastructure, but as a living system capable of supporting learning, creativity, autonomy, relationships, wellbeing, and quality of life throughout the entire human lifespan.
