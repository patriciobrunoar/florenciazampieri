The Artist

Statement

I am an artist who transforms spaces, an architect who is inspired through movement, a dancer who draws, light, in the air, when she rises. The canvas is a pentagram, which allows me to listen, perceive and connect with other canvases, other emotions. Art in me is inevitable, it is magic that materializes the human essence, the transforming alchemy of the body, spirit and soul.

The structure was my starting point, my axis of balance for dancing and composing. I learned to dematerialize without losing harmony, always attracted by the creative discipline and experimentation, the plastic, abstract and geometric language of the Bauhaus School but at the same time, poetic, appealing to metaphor and the re-signification of the element and form. I am inspired by places I visit, cities near water, I am inspired by the beauty of calm, the nature of simplicity, textures and their depths, subjective aesthetics, the magic of the unexpected, the books I read. I don’t find limits between my perceptions, I think they intertwine with each other and allow the fluidity I need to create.

I seek to inhabit beauty, let myself be carried away by the unexpected, materialize sound and emotions, create affinity with the other, generate movement to whom my art reaches in the depths of its essence.

Biography

Florencia Zampieri

Architect · Neuroarchitect · Visual Artist · Wellbeing Researcher · University Professor · Strategic Facilitator · Founder of The Blue Maker

Florencia Zampieri is an Architect and Urban Planner, Neuroarchitect, Visual Artist, University Professor, and Wellbeing Researcher whose work explores the intersection of architecture, neuroscience, environmental psychology, art, color, learning, human development, and longevity.

With more than 30 years of professional and academic experience, she investigates how the built environment influences human perception, behavior, emotional experiences, learning, creativity, and wellbeing. Her interdisciplinary approach translates scientific knowledge into strategies for designing environments that support human potential and quality of life throughout the lifespan.

She holds a degree in Architecture and Urban Planning from the University of Buenos Aires and postgraduate specializations in Neuroarchitecture, Cognitive Neuroscience, and Environmental Psychology. Since 1996, she has worked as a university professor and lecturer at academic, cultural, and professional institutions in Argentina and internationally.

Her multidisciplinary background also includes formal training in Classical Dance, which informs her understanding of the relationships between body, movement, spatial perception, sensory experience, and human expression. She has further training in Coaching and LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY® methodology, applying these approaches to strategic facilitation, innovation, participatory design, learning processes, and organizational transformation.

The Blue Maker: Research, Methodology, and Innovation

Florencia is the founder and creator of The Blue Maker, an interdisciplinary ecosystem for research, education, consulting, and innovation at the intersection of architecture, neuroscience, color, art, wellbeing, and longevity.

Through The Blue Maker, she develops methodologies, educational programs, consulting services, and design strategies that explore how environments can respond more effectively to human cognitive, emotional, sensory, social, and biological needs.

She is the creator of the MINC Method and develops research into Neuro-Compatible Spaces, advancing a human-centered approach to designing and transforming environments for living, learning, working, and aging.

Her work also encompasses concepts such as Blue Ageless, Learning Architecture, Neuroarts, and the relationship between color, perception, and wellbeing. Her research into the color blue explores its potential as a sensory, spatial, and emotional dimension of human experience.

Her vision extends beyond the conventional understanding of architecture as physical infrastructure, positioning the built environment as an essential component of human development, wellbeing, and quality of life.

Conscious Longevity and the Future of Living

Florencia is an active advocate and communicator of the Conscious Longevity Movement, promoting initiatives inspired by quality of life, active aging, and international research conducted in collaboration with global partners.

Her work connects urban planning, architecture, and future-oriented design with the evolving needs of longevity-oriented populations and the Silver Economy. She explores innovative approaches to age-inclusive environments, community wellbeing, autonomy, social connection, and the design of places that support people throughout the life course.

She is a Founding Member of the Alianza Latinoamericana para la Innovación de la Longevidad (ALIL), contributing to collaborative initiatives and innovative perspectives on longevity, human development, wellbeing, and the future of aging in Latin America.

Her strategic vision of human development, talent management, and the future of work also led her to become Co-Founder of Talent Ally Group, an initiative focused on recognizing and developing innate talent and fostering more human-centered organizational cultures.

Art, Culture, and International Engagement

As a visual artist, Florencia has participated in exhibitions and projects in Argentina and internationally. Her artistic practice brings together color, perception, nature, sensory experience, and scientific inquiry, creating a distinctive dialogue between artistic expression and applied knowledge.

She serves as LATAM Ambassador for Arts & Culture for Living Blue Zone, representing the organization in the Latin American region and contributing to the dissemination of its principles and values through artistic, cultural, and scientific communication.

Her work connects art, architecture, and longevity, exploring how creative practices and thoughtfully designed environments can contribute to more meaningful, connected, and wellbeing-oriented communities.

Her artistic and research interests include the exploration of blue as a spatial, sensory, and emotional experience, bringing together visual language, environmental perception, and the relationship between people and the spaces they inhabit.

Research, Teaching, and Professional Practice

Florencia works as a consultant, researcher, educator, lecturer, and scientific and cultural communicator. She presents publications, academic papers, lectures, and articles through national and international platforms, contributing to discussions on neuroarchitecture, color, environmental psychology, wellbeing, human development, and longevity.

She is a member of the European Society of Medicine (ESMED), the Academy of Neuroscience for Architecture (ANFA), Diseñadores de Interiores Argentinos Asociados (DARA), Sociedad Argentina de Medicina del Estrés (SAMES) and Interdisciplinary Colour Association Belgium (ICA Belgium); Vocal of the Grupo Argentino del Color (GAC); and member of the Red Latinoamericana de Color. As a visual artist she has been certified in the USA and named Golden Ambassador of the World Academy of Woman Artists, and was a Finalist in The Global Art Awards 2018, Five Palm Jumeirah, Dubai, in the Painting category (November 2018).

Her professional practice brings together scientific inquiry, artistic exploration, education, and strategic consulting to develop new approaches to spatial design, human-centered innovation, learning, and longevity.

Her vision is to transform the way we design, inhabit, learn, work, and age, understanding architecture and culture as powerful resources for supporting human potential, autonomy, creativity, connection, and quality of life throughout the lifespan.
