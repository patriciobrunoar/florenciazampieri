About

Florencia Zampieri

I am an artist who transforms spaces, an architect who is inspired through movement, a dancer who draws light in the air when she rises. The canvas is a pentagram, which allows me to listen, perceive and connect with other canvases, other emotions. Art in me is inevitable — it is magic that materialises the human essence, the transforming alchemy of body, spirit and soul.

I am inspired by places I visit, cities near water, the beauty of calm, the nature of simplicity, textures and their depths. I don’t find limits between my perceptions — I think they intertwine, and allow the fluidity I need to create.

Institutional & Academic Profile

Urban Architect · Neuroarchitect · Visual Artist · University Educator and Researcher in Wellbeing and Healthy Environments. Florencia Zampieri graduated as an Urban Architect from the Universidad de Buenos Aires (UBA), with three decades of experience in university teaching, research and the development of projects focused on experience design, environmental perception and quality of life. Her work sits at the interdisciplinary forefront, integrating architecture, art, cognitive neuroscience and environmental psychology to promote environments that support people’s physical, emotional and social health.

As a pioneer in science-based design, she is the founder of The Blue Maker, an ecosystem connecting knowledge, design and creativity, through which she develops consultancy, training and research in neuroarchitecture, neuroaesthetics and colour. In keeping with her commitment to environmental health, she has expanded her work as the creator of Blue Ageless and of the Blue Mind Lifestyle and Blue Nodes initiatives, projects designed to raise awareness of the impact of environmental stimuli on the brain and human wellbeing, and she proposes studying longevity as a continuous process of human development.

She is also an active advocate for the Conscious Longevity Movement, driving programmes inspired by quality of life and active ageing (based on research with her international partners), linking urban planning and the design of the future with the needs of longevity populations and the silver economy. Her strategic vision on human development, talent management and the workplace also led her to become co-founder of Talent Ally Group.

Since 1996, she has worked as a university lecturer and speaker at prestigious academic, cultural and professional institutions in Argentina and abroad. Her postgraduate training includes specialisations in Neuroarchitecture, Cognitive Neuroscience and Environmental Psychology. With a strong focus on learning architecture, strategic facilitation and people development, she has complemented her profile with a specialisation in Coaching and in the LEGO® Serious Play® methodology, key tools she applies in innovation processes, participatory design and spatial and organisational transformation dynamics.

As a visual artist, she has taken part in national and international exhibitions, achieving a unique crossover between artistic expression, applied science and the study of colour. Internationally certified, she was named Arts & Culture Ambassador for Latin America, representing the European organisation The Living Blue Zones, for her work and commitment to the scientific and artistic dissemination of its principles and values.

She currently works as a consultant and speaker, presenting publications, papers and articles in various national and international media as a member of the European Society of Medicine and Academy of Neuroscience for Architecture.
