SenseCity™ — Cities for Healthy Longevity

Sensitive cities for long, healthy and full lives. An innovative model that integrates Sustainability, Regeneration and Human Sensibility into 21st-century urban design.

sostenible

Cities that are socially and economically resilient.

sustentable

Cities that regenerate their ecosystems and natural resources.

sensible

Cities that understand and respond to human needs.

We design cities that care for people at every stage of life.

The 7 SenseCity™ Domains

Seven domains, one integrated city

  • 1. Health & Longevity. We promote healthy lifestyles and disease-preventing environments.
  • 2. Lifelong Learning. We foster cognitive development and education throughout life.
  • 3. Built Environment. We create accessible, walkable, safe and age-friendly spaces.
  • 4. Nature & Regeneration. We reconnect people with nature and restore ecosystems.
  • 5. Community & Purpose. We strengthen bonds, reduce loneliness and cultivate a sense of purpose.
  • 6. Culture & Senses. We drive creativity, identity and emotional wellbeing.
  • 7. Human Innovation. We use technology to enhance capabilities and improve quality of life.

The 3S® Method

sensitise

Understand people and territories through empathy, active listening and scientific evidence.

synergise

Integrate multidisciplinary knowledge, promote collaboration and co-create innovative solutions.

sustain

Implement measurable actions, evaluate impact and ensure continuity for long-term wellbeing.

A practical, transformative method for designing cities that care today and endure through time.

Implementation

The SenseCity™ Route

  • 01 — Integral Diagnosis. We assess the city’s current situation.
  • 02 — Longevity Map. We identify strengths, risks and opportunities.
  • 03 — Participatory Co-Design. We build solutions together with the community.
  • 04 — Pilot Projects. We implement and test innovative initiatives.
  • 05 — Scaling & Integration. We incorporate them into public policy and strategic plans.
  • 06 — SenseCity Certification. We evaluate results and recognise progress.
