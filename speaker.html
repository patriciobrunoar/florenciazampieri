Speaking & Facilitation

Ideas that move the room

For close to three decades Florencia has taught, trained and facilitated — translating research on colour, sensory perception and neuroarchitecture into talks and workshops for design schools, cultural institutions and corporate teams.

  • Keynote

    Neuroarchitecture & Wellbeing

    How the built environment shapes cognition, emotion and long-term health — and what that means for the spaces we design and inhabit.

  • Talk

    Blue Mind: Colour, Water & the Psychology of Calm

    The science and the art behind why proximity to blue and to water restores focus, drawing on Florencia’s own Blue Life research and practice.

  • Workshop

    LEGO® Serious Play® Facilitation

    Certified facilitator (Rasmussen Consulting, Denmark, 2018) leading teams through hands-on strategic and creative problem-solving.

  • Workshop

    Colour Planning & Sensory Retail Experience

    Deco-store colour styling and visual merchandising training for design and retail professionals.
