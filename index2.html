Proyecto · Blue Mind Life Style

The Blue Maker

Blue Mind Life Style is Florencia's ongoing body of work — painting, textile and interior objects built around a single conviction: that proximity to blue and to water measurably restores calm, focus and wellbeing. Each collection below approaches that idea from a different sense.

mind

Relax · Peace · Creativity · Cheerful · Freedom — the emotional register of the work, in acrylic and watercolour.

body

Wool and woven pieces — Okinawa, Barbagia, Loma Linda, Icaria, Nicoya — named for the world's Blue Zones of longevity.

spirit

Pacífico, Ártico, Índico, Atlántico, Antártico — large-scale textured canvases named for the oceans that inspire them.

soul

Sunlight, Twilight, Midlight, Abyss, Freedom — the deepest, most introspective works in the collection.

Research

The Blue Senses

Blue Senses is Florencia's research thread linking anthroposophic sense theory, neuroscience and environmental psychology to her studio practice — inhabiting the sense, and making the invisible visible.

Her academic specialisation in Neuroarchitecture, Cognitive Neuroscience & Environmental Psychology (INESEM, Granada, 2023–24) sits alongside a Customer Experience Management certification (FIU, Florida International University, 2016–17) and three decades of applied research at FADU‑UBA.

About

Florencia Zampieri

I am an artist who transforms spaces, an architect who is inspired through movement, a dancer who draws light in the air when she rises. The canvas is a pentagram, which allows me to listen, perceive and connect with other canvases, other emotions. Art in me is inevitable — it is magic that materialises the human essence, the transforming alchemy of body, spirit and soul.

I am inspired by places I visit, cities near water, the beauty of calm, the nature of simplicity, textures and their depths. I don't find limits between my perceptions — I think they intertwine, and allow the fluidity I need to create.
